Delete your CoachPlan account

Version 2026-09-14 · 14 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your CoachPlan account and what happens to your data. CoachPlan is provided by Sotirios Panagiotis Koulouridis.

1. Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription and does not stop billing. If you are a coach with a subscription, cancel it first in Google Play: Play Store → your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → CoachPlan → Cancel subscription.

2. Delete from the app

Open the menu → Settings → Privacy → Delete Account, then confirm. You may be asked to sign in again.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

3. Delete without the app

If you can no longer use the app, email CoachPlan@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it and confirm within one month.

4. What is deleted

For every account: your profile and profile photo, your sign-in account, your consent records and your usage records for the automated features.

If you are a coach: the profiles, measurements, calorie and meal logs, session feedback and workout and meal plans of all your trainees, plus your preset plans, appointments, the messages you sent and your subscription status record.

If you are a trainee: your measurements, calorie and meal logs, session feedback and the messages sent to you. Sessions you had booked are freed and your link to your coach is removed.

5. What is kept

If you are a trainee, your coach keeps the client record they created for you, including the plans and details stored on it, with no link to your account. To have that removed as well, ask your coach or contact CoachPlan@gmail.com.

Purchase records held by Google Play and by RevenueCat, our subscription provider, are not removed when you delete your account.

Crash reports are not linked to your account and are deleted automatically by Firebase after 90 days.

Backups are overwritten on their normal cycle.
