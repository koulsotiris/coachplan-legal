Privacy Policy

Version 2026-09-10 · 10 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data CoachPlan collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. It covers both coaches and trainees.

1. Who we are, and who is responsible for what

Controller: Sotirios Panagiotis Koulouridis. For any privacy question, or to exercise any right described below, contact CoachPlan@gmail.com.

We are the controller for your account: your name, email, role, language, profile photo, subscription status, device notification token and crash diagnostics. We are also the controller for the health and fitness data you enter about yourself.

Where a coach records information about a trainee, the coach decides what to record and why. For that information the coach is a controller and we act on their behalf as their processor. In practice this means your coach answers for what they chose to write down about you, and we answer for how the app stores and protects it.

You do not have to work out which of us to ask. Contact CoachPlan@gmail.com about anything in this policy and we will deal with it or pass it to your coach and tell you we have done so.

2. What we collect

Account data: your name, email address, chosen language, whether you are a coach or a trainee, and a profile photo if you upload one.

Health and fitness data: age, sex, height, weight, target weight, body fat percentage, goals, general health status, current and previous injuries, body measurements, calorie and meal logs, and post-workout feedback including reported pain and the exercises that caused it.

Coaching data: workout and meal plans, session notes, appointments and bookings, and messages your coach sends you.

Technical data: a push notification token for your device, and crash diagnostics if the app fails.

Subscription data: which plan a coach is on and its status. We never receive your card or bank details. Payment is handled entirely by the app store.

3. Health data needs your explicit consent

Most of what CoachPlan holds about a trainee is data concerning health. Under Article 9 of the GDPR this is a special category of personal data that we may only process with your explicit consent.

That is why health-data consent is asked for separately from these documents, and why it can be withdrawn on its own. You are never required to accept it as part of accepting anything else.

If you withdraw it, we stop processing your health data and you will no longer be able to use the tracking features. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already lawfully took place.

4. Why we process your data, and on what basis

To provide the service, which covers creating your account, storing your plans, showing your coach your progress and sending appointment reminders. Basis: performance of our contract with you.

To process health and fitness information. Basis: your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)).

To generate automated nutrition estimates and coaching suggestions. Basis: your explicit consent, as this uses health data.

To take payment and enforce plan limits. Basis: performance of contract.

To keep the service secure and diagnose faults. Basis: our legitimate interest in a working, secure product.

To meet legal obligations, including keeping a record that you consented.

5. Automated processing

CoachPlan sends some of your information to OpenAI to produce estimated calorie and macronutrient figures and short coaching suggestions. For coaching suggestions this includes weight, target weight, body fat, stated goals, health status and injury descriptions.

These outputs are advisory. No decision producing a legal or similarly significant effect is made about you automatically. Your coach decides what to do, and you decide whether to follow it.

We do not permit this data to be used to train OpenAI's models.

6. Who your data is shared with

Your coach, if you are a trainee: they can see the profile, plans, logs, measurements and session feedback associated with you. This is the purpose of the app.

Google Ireland / Google LLC (Firebase): authentication, database, file storage, server functions, push notifications and crash reporting.

OpenAI: the automated estimates described above.

RevenueCat and your app store: subscription status and payment.

We do not sell your personal data or share it with advertisers.

7. Transfers outside the EEA

Some of these providers process data in the United States. Those transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, and where applicable the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, together with the providers' own technical safeguards.

You can ask us at CoachPlan@gmail.com for details of the safeguards in place.

8. How long we keep it

Calorie and meal logs: 90 days, then deleted automatically.

Post-workout session feedback, including pain reports: 180 days, then deleted automatically.

Appointment records: 35 days after the week they belong to.

Messages from your coach: 24 hours.

Account data, profile, plans and measurements: until you delete your account.

Consent records: kept while your account exists, as evidence that we were entitled to process your data. They are deleted with your account.

9. Deleting your account

Settings → Privacy → Delete account removes your account and its data.

For a coach this deletes their trainees' profiles, plans, logs, measurements, session feedback, appointments and messages.

For a trainee this deletes their logs, measurements and session feedback, frees any sessions they had booked, and unlinks them from their coach. The coach keeps the business record of the client they created, without any link to a live account.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Backups are overwritten on their normal cycle.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have data erased, to restrict or object to processing, to receive your data in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time.

Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it: Settings → Privacy → Consent. Withdrawing does not make earlier processing unlawful.

To exercise any right, contact CoachPlan@gmail.com. We respond within one month.

If you believe we have handled your data improperly you may complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr), or to the supervisory authority where you live.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our infrastructure provider. Access rules restrict each account to its own data, and requests are verified as coming from a genuine installation of the app.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify you and the supervisory authority as the GDPR requires.

12. Children

CoachPlan is not intended for anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact CoachPlan@gmail.com and we will delete it.

13. Where your data comes from

Most of what we hold comes from you: what you type into the app and what you choose to log.

If you are a trainee, some of it comes from your coach instead. A coach invites you by email address, and can record your measurements, training history, injuries and session notes. Your coach may have entered some of this before you created an account, which is why you may see information you did not type yourself.

Before adding you, your coach has to confirm in the app that they have your permission and have told you the data will be stored here. If that is not true, tell us at CoachPlan@gmail.com and we will investigate and, where appropriate, remove the data and suspend the account.

We do not buy personal data and we do not collect it from public sources, data brokers or social networks.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your data in a way that affects your rights, we will ask you to review and accept the new version in the app. The version and date at the top tell you which text applies.
